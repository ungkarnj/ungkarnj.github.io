  <div style="flex: 2; min-width: 280px;">
    <h3>Smartphone-Based Platform for Micro- and Nanoparticle Sizing</h3>
    <p><em>Keywords: Nanoparticle Sizing • Differential Dynamic Microscopy • Point-of-Care Diagnostics</em></p>
    <p>Micro- and nanoparticle sizing plays a vital role in fields such as medical diagnostics, environmental monitoring, and nanomaterial synthesis. Conventional differential dynamic microscopy (DDM) systems remain bulky, expensive, and unable to resolve multi-modal size distributions. We developed a compact, low-cost smartphone-based DDM platform utilizing a reversed lens and 3D-printed housing to create a portable microscope for non-destructive, label-free particle sizing. Coupled with non-negative least squares algorithms, the system accurately measures particles down to <strong>30 nm</strong> from a 10 µL sample within 4 minutes.</p>
    <p><strong>Related Publication:</strong><br>
    <strong>Jarujareet, U.</strong>, & Koonyosying, P. (2026). A smartphone platform for portable and cost-effective micro- and nanoparticle sizing. <em>Sensors and Actuators A: Physical</em>, 117372. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sna.2025.117372" target="_blank">DOI: 10.1016/j.sna.2025.117372</a></p>

    <h3>Beta-Thalassemia Variant Classification via Deep Learning</h3>
    <p><em>Keywords: Deep Learning • Medical Imaging • Hematology • Thalassemia</em></p>
    <p>We introduced the <strong>Image Structure Function Image (ISFI)</strong> of whole blood as a novel spatial-temporal feature representation for blood disease characterization. By processing dynamic light fluctuations with deep learning architectures, this approach enables automated classification of severe thalassemia variants (including $\beta$-thalassemia major and $\text{HbE}/\beta$-thalassemia), supporting rapid diagnostic triage in clinical settings.</p>
    <p><strong>Related Publication:</strong><br>
    <strong>Jarujareet, U.</strong>, Wiratchawa, K., Petiwathayakorn, T., Koonyosying, P., Hantrakool, S., Srichairatanakool, S., & Intharah, T. (2025). Classification of beta-thalassemia major and HbE/beta-thalassemia via deep learning of image structure function image. <em>Biomedical Signal Processing and Control</em>, 102, 107265. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bspc.2024.107265" target="_blank">DOI: 10.1016/j.bspc.2024.107265</a></p>

    <h3>Portable Devices for Microrheological Characterization</h3>
    <p><em>Keywords: Microrheology • Bio-fluid Dynamics • Hypercoagulability • Biomaterials</em></p>
    <p>We designed portable optical instrumentation to measure viscoelastic parameters (such as storage/loss modulus and shear viscosity) in micro-volume biological fluids. Applications include early detection of hypercoagulable states in transfusion-dependent patients and evaluating artificial saliva formulations for dentin remineralization.</p>
